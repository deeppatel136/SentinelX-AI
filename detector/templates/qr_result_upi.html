UPI Payment Safety Tips

Always Do

  • Verify the receiver's name before payment.
  • Confirm the UPI ID carefully.
  • Check the payment amount.
  • Pay only trusted merchants or individuals.
  • Review the transaction details inside your UPI app.
  • Use SentinelX AI before scanning unknown QR codes.

Never Do

  • Never scan a QR code to receive money.
  • Never trust QR codes sent by unknown people.
  • Never share your UPI PIN.
  • Never ignore receiver details.
  • Never pay under pressure or urgency.
  • Never trust lottery, cashback or reward QR codes.
