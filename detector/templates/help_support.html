Frequently Asked Questions

SentinelX AI provides multiple security scanning features. You can analyze suspicious website URLs, email content, images containing suspicious text, files and documents, and QR codes. Each scanner uses different security checks depending on the type of content being analyzed.

SentinelX AI combines rule-based security analysis with machine learning and AI-powered explanations. The platform checks available security indicators such as suspicious patterns, unusual structures, risky keywords, phishing techniques, and other known threat characteristics. Multiple indicators are considered together to provide a clearer assessment of the submitted content.

Your previous scan activity can be viewed from the Scan History section in the SentinelX AI sidebar. The history is associated with your authenticated account and allows you to review previously scanned URLs, emails, files, images, and QR codes. You can also use available filters to find specific types of scan results more easily.

No automated security system can guarantee that every possible threat will always be detected. A Safe result means SentinelX AI did not identify significant threat indicators during the available analysis. You should still use good security practices, especially when dealing with unknown websites, unexpected attachments, login requests, payment instructions, or messages asking for personal information.

Scan history is associated with your authenticated SentinelX AI account so that you can review your previous security analysis results. However, you should avoid submitting highly sensitive information such as passwords, authentication codes, banking credentials, or other private credentials into any scanner unless the platform specifically requests and securely supports that information.
