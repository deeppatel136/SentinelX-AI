WiFi Security Recommendations

Recommended security practices for wireless networks

Recommended
  • Connect only to trusted WiFi networks.
  • Prefer WPA2 or WPA3 encrypted networks.
  • Verify the network name before connecting.
  • Use a VPN on public WiFi.
  • Disable Auto-Connect for unknown networks.
  • Keep your operating system updated.
Avoid
  • Avoid open WiFi networks whenever possible.
  • Never enter banking credentials on public WiFi.
  • Avoid unknown hotspot names.
  • Do not ignore certificate warnings.
  • Don't share WiFi passwords publicly.
  • Disconnect when the network is no longer needed.
